WordPro Editorial Team

Lead Writers & Fact-Checkers

Our editorial team is made up of competitive Scrabble and Wordle players who have played in NASPA-sanctioned club games and use TWL and Collins Scrabble Words dictionaries daily. Every piece of content we publish reflects hands-on word game knowledge — not recycled tips from other sites.

We verify every word claim against at least one major competitive dictionary (TWL06 or CSW/SOWPODS) and clearly state which dictionaries each word applies to. Scrabble tile scores in our guides always use official NASPA values.

Content is reviewed before publication and updated whenever official dictionaries release new word additions or remove words. Errors notified via our Contact page are corrected within 5 business days.

Our Editorial Standards

  • All word validity claims verified in TWL and/or Collins Scrabble Words
  • Scrabble scores use official NASPA tile values only
  • Guides written from competitive play experience, not aggregated from other sites
  • Dictionary coverage stated explicitly — US TWL, UK SOWPODS, or both
  • Content updated when official dictionaries are revised
  • No paid placement in word lists or guides — all content is editorially independent

What We Cover

Our team maintains four content areas on this site: the Strategy Guides covering Scrabble, Wordle, Words With Friends, and Boggle; the Word Lists organised by length, starting letter, and pattern; the Blog for shorter technique tips; and the word unscrambler tool itself, which is updated whenever the underlying dictionaries change.

We also maintain over 200 pre-solved letter combination pages (see all word pages) that show every valid word you can build from specific sets of letters, scored and sorted for competitive play.

Corrections & Updates

If you spot a word that should be in our tool but isn't, a strategy tip that's wrong, or a scoring error in a guide, please contact us. We take accuracy seriously and investigate every report. Our full editorial policy is available on the Editorial Standards page.
