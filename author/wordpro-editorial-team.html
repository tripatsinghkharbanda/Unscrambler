Who We Are

The WordPro Editorial Team is the group of word game specialists, linguists, and developers who research, write, and validate all content published on Unscramble Words Pro. We do not publish content under individual bylines because our work is inherently collaborative — every guide is drafted, fact-checked against official dictionaries, and reviewed by multiple team members before it goes live.

Our team draws on a mix of backgrounds: competitive Scrabble players who have studied the TWL and SOWPODS word lists in depth, crossword constructors who understand how letter patterns and word structure work at scale, and software engineers who built the underlying word-finding engine. That combination gives our content an accuracy and depth that purely editorial teams rarely achieve on their own.

We are based across North America and the United Kingdom, which means we cover both the TWL (Tournament Word List, used in US and Canadian Scrabble) and Collins Scrabble Words / SOWPODS (used in UK, Australian, and international play) equally well. No regional bias, no gaps.

Our Expertise

  • Competitive Scrabble & Word Games: Members of the team have studied the full TWL and SOWPODS word lists, including all valid two-letter words, bingo-frequency seven-letter stems (SATINE, TISANE, RETINA), and high-value Q, X, Z, and J plays. Our strategy guides are written from this competitive perspective, not from a casual how-to angle.
  • Crossword Construction & Word Pattern Analysis: Understanding how words interlock — hooks, parallel plays, extensions — requires the same skills used in crossword construction. Our team applies this structural knowledge to Scrabble board analysis and to understanding why certain word lengths and letter combinations matter.
  • Computational Linguistics & Dictionary Systems: Our word-finding engine processes three dictionary sources in parallel (ENABLE, Collins SOWPODS, and the dwyl English word list) and returns scored results in under 100 milliseconds. The team maintains this system, audits it against official rulings, and updates it when dictionaries change.
  • Vocabulary Acquisition & Study Methods: Beyond gameplay, the team has researched evidence-based vocabulary learning — spaced repetition, high-frequency word clusters, and pattern-based memorisation. This informs guides like our word endings reference and the Q-without-U words list.
  • International English Spelling: Our content covers US, UK, Canadian, and Australian spelling variants because all four regions play official word games and use subtly different dictionaries. Team members are native speakers of both American and British English.

How We Create Content

Every piece of content on this site follows the same production process:

01

Research & Sourcing

We start from official dictionary sources — TWL, Collins Scrabble Words, ENABLE — and cross-reference with competitive Scrabble community resources and peer-reviewed linguistics research where applicable.

02

Algorithmic Validation

Word lists are generated programmatically from our 370,000-word dictionary engine, then reviewed manually. Any word on the site can be traced to at least one official source.

03

All guides are written by team members with direct subject expertise, then reviewed by a second team member for accuracy, clarity, and factual completeness before publication.

04

Ongoing Maintenance

Word game dictionaries are updated periodically. We monitor TWL and Collins CSW update cycles and revise affected pages within 30 days of any official dictionary change.

What Makes Our Tools Accurate

Accuracy in word games is binary — a word is either valid or it is not, and the stakes matter when you are playing competitively. We take this seriously. Here is how we ensure our tool and content are correct:

  • Three-dictionary coverage: We combine ENABLE (~173,000 words, standard for North American word games), Collins SOWPODS (~280,000 words, used in UK, Australian, and international Scrabble), and the dwyl English word list (~370,000 entries). Any word valid in at least one major dictionary is included in our results.
  • Scrabble point validation: Every word is scored using the official Scrabble tile values (A=1, B=3, C=3, D=2, E=1, F=4, G=2, H=4, I=1, J=8, K=5, L=1, M=3, N=1, O=1, P=3, Q=10, R=1, S=1, T=1, U=1, V=4, W=4, X=8, Y=4, Z=10). Scores are calculated without board premium squares so they represent the base word value.
  • Proper noun exclusion: Our dictionary processing pipeline filters proper nouns at the source level, so results contain only valid common-word entries. This is consistent with official Scrabble rules in both TWL and SOWPODS jurisdictions.
  • Regular audits: We run automated consistency checks against our dictionary sources on a quarterly basis and act on any discrepancies found.
