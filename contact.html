Reporting a Missing or Invalid Word

Our word finder checks three dictionaries in parallel: the ENABLE word list (~173,000 words, standard for US word games), Collins SOWPODS (~280,000 words, UK and international Scrabble), and the dwyl English word list (~370,000 words). Together these cover virtually all valid English words.

If you believe a word is missing or incorrectly included, please include the following in your email so we can investigate efficiently:

  • The word in question
  • Which game you were playing (Scrabble TWL, Scrabble SOWPODS, Words With Friends, Wordle)
  • Whether the word was rejected by our tool or by your game app
  • Any reference source you used to verify it (official Scrabble dictionary, Merriam-Webster, Collins, etc.)

Note that our tool shows words valid in at least one major dictionary. Some words appear in SOWPODS but not TWL, or vice versa. The TWL vs SOWPODS guide explains these differences in detail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Unscramble Words Pro free to use?
Yes — completely free, no account required. The word finder runs entirely in your browser. After your first visit the full 370,000-word dictionary is cached locally, so subsequent searches work instantly even offline.
What dictionaries does the tool use?
We combine three sources: ENABLE (~173k words, standard for US Scrabble), Collins SOWPODS (~280k words, UK and international Scrabble), and the dwyl English word list (~370k general English words). Every result is valid in at least one major word game dictionary.
Can I use this tool for Wordle?
Yes. Set the word length to 5, enter your confirmed letters, and use the starts-with, ends-with, or contains filters to match your Wordle clues. All matching five-letter words are shown instantly. See our Wordle tips guide for strategy.
Do you share user data with third parties?
We do not sell or share personal data. Letters you enter into the word finder are processed entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to our servers. We use Google Analytics (anonymised) and Google AdSense. See our full Privacy Policy for details.

About Unscramble Words Pro

Unscramble Words Pro is a free online word finder and Scrabble solver serving players across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. We cover both the TWL (US/Canada) and Collins SOWPODS (UK/Australia/International) Scrabble dictionaries, giving players in every English-speaking country accurate results for tournament and casual play.

Our guides section covers Scrabble strategy, Wordle tips, Words With Friends tactics, and vocabulary building. Our word lists cover 5-letter words by starting letter, word-length hubs, and high-value Scrabble lists for Q, X, and Z words. Everything is free and updated regularly.
