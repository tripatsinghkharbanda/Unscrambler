Unscramble ALERT

ALERT contains 5 letters: A, L, E, R, T. These letters form 5 complete anagrams (ALTER, LATER, RATEL, TALER, ARTEL) plus over 20 shorter words. The A+L+E+R+T letter set is one of Scrabble's most versatile 5-tile combinations because all five letters are common and low-point, giving you maximum placement flexibility.

What Words Can You Make From ALERT?

The letters A, L, E, R, T form five valid 5-letter anagrams: ALTER (5 pts), LATER (5 pts), RATEL (5 pts, a honey badger), TALER (5 pts, a silver coin), and ARTEL (5 pts, a workers' cooperative—SOWPODS). In competitive Scrabble, knowing all five anagrams of your rack means you can always find the best board position regardless of where the letters physically fit.

Four-letter words from ALERT: LATE (4 pts), TALE (4 pts), RATE (4 pts), REAL (4 pts), TEAR (4 pts), TARE (4 pts), EARL (4 pts), TEAL (4 pts), RALE (4 pts), LEAR (4 pts), ARET (4 pts, SOWPODS). Three-letter words: ALE, ARE, ART, ATE, EAR, EAT, ERA, ETA, LAT, LEA, LET, RAT, TAR, TAE, TEA (all 3 pts). Two-letter words: AE, AL, AR, AT, EL, ER, ET, LA, RE, TA.

ALERT as a Scrabble Rack Stem

ALERT is one of the most studied 5-tile Scrabble stems because all five letters are high-frequency. Adding a 6th tile creates dozens of 6-letter words: ALERTS (6 pts), ALTERS (6 pts), ARTELS (SOWPODS), LATERS. Adding a 7th tile reaches bingo territory: ALERTLY, ALERTED, RELATEDLY. Players who hold ALERT should focus on finding the best available 6th tile on the board to play through rather than settling for a 5-letter play.

ALERT Letters in Wordle

ALERT is a strong Wordle guess itself—testing A, L, E, R, T in positions 1–5. These five letters appear in the top 10 most common Wordle letters. Many experienced Wordle players use ALERT as their second guess after an opener like SONIC or GROUT, covering the vowel A and the consonants L, R, T that their opener missed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What words can you make from the letters ALERT?
5-letter anagrams: ALTER, LATER, RATEL, TALER, ARTEL (SOWPODS). 4-letter: LATE, TALE, RATE, REAL, TEAR, TARE, EARL, TEAL, RALE, LEAR. 3-letter: ALE, ARE, ART, ATE, EAR, EAT, ERA, ETA, LAT, LEA, LET, RAT, TAR, TEA. 2-letter: AE, AL, AR, AT, EL, ER, ET, LA, RE, TA.
Is ALERT a good Wordle guess?
Yes. ALERT tests A (very common), L, E (most common English letter), R, and T—five of the top 10 most frequent letters in Wordle answers. Many players use ALERT as their second guess after an opener that covers other common letters (like SONIC covering S, O, N, I, C).
What is RATEL in Scrabble?
RATEL is a honey badger (Mellivora capensis), valid in both TWL and SOWPODS. It is one of five 5-letter anagrams of ALERT/ALTER/LATER and scores 5 pts. It is worth memorising because opponents rarely challenge animal names when they are obscure but valid.
How many anagrams does ALERT have?
ALERT has exactly 5 valid 5-letter anagrams: ALTER, LATER, RATEL, TALER, and ARTEL (SOWPODS only). All score 5 base points. Knowing all five means you can always find the best board position for this tile set regardless of available lanes.
What 6-letter words can I make by adding a tile to ALERT?
ALERTS and ALTERS (+S), ALERTED (+D), ALERTLY (+Y), ARTELS (+S, SOWPODS), TILLERS—add S, D, or common tiles to extend ALERT into 6-letter plays. The ALERT stem is particularly valuable because all five tiles are high-frequency, making 6-letter plays easier to find.
