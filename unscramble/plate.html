Unscramble PLATE

PLATE contains 5 letters: P, L, A, T, E. These form 4 valid 5-letter anagrams: PLATE, LEAPT, PETAL, and PLEAT (TEPAL is also valid in SOWPODS). All score 7 base points in Scrabble. The ALE vowel core (A+L+E) makes this set highly versatile—17+ shorter words are formable including common 4-letter plays LATE, PALE, TALE, TAPE, PEAL, and TEAL.

The PLATE Anagram Family

PLATE's four (or five with TEPAL) anagrams illustrate an important Scrabble principle: when you recognise that your rack forms a specific anagram family, you are no longer constrained to one word orientation. PLATE and PLEAT both place P at the start; LEAPT leads with L; PETAL starts with P+E. Choose whichever fits the available board lane best—they all score identically.

TEPAL (a leaf-like part of a flower, 7 pts, SOWPODS) is the fifth member of this family for SOWPODS players. Knowing TEPAL gives international tournament players an additional orientation option, particularly useful on late-game boards where PLATE/PLEAT/LEAPT/PETAL have no available lanes.

PLATE in Wordle Strategy

PLATE is a solid Wordle guess, testing P, L, A, T, E—five common letters with two vowels. LEAPT and PLEAT cover the same letters and are equally valid guesses. Many Wordle players find PLATE useful as a second guess when their opener confirmed A or E but not both. If your first guess showed green A and yellow E, PLATE places E in a new position while keeping A.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are all the anagrams of PLATE?
PLATE, PLEAT, LEAPT, and PETAL are the four main anagrams (all valid in TWL and SOWPODS, each scoring 7 pts). TEPAL is a fifth anagram valid in SOWPODS only. All five use the same P+L+A+T+E tiles in different arrangements.
Is LEAPT a valid Scrabble word?
Yes. LEAPT is the past tense of LEAP, valid in both TWL and SOWPODS. It scores 7 pts and is one of four main anagrams of PLATE. It is particularly useful when the board has a lane starting with L that PLATE and PLEAT (both P-initial) cannot use.
What 4-letter words can I make from PLATE?
LATE, TALE, PEAL, TEAL, TAPE, PALE, PLEA, LEAP, PEAT, and LEPT are all valid 4-letter words from PLATE. These shorter options are valuable when the board has no 5-letter lane available—PALE (6 pts) and PEAL (6 pts) in particular score well for their length.
Is PLATE a good Wordle guess?
Yes. PLATE tests P, L, A, T, E—five common letters with vowels A and E. PLEAT and LEAPT cover identical letters. If your Wordle opener showed A or E yellow or green, PLATE efficiently tests a new position for that vowel while covering P, L, and T.
What 6-letter words extend PLATE?
PLATES (+S), PLATED (+D), PLATEN (+N, a printer part), PALATE (+A second), STAPLE (rearranged with S), PETALS (+S). Add S or D to make 6-letter plays. PLATED and PLATEN are particularly useful when the board has an open lane that PLATE itself cannot reach.
