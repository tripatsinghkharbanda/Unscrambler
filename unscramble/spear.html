Unscramble SPEAR

SPEAR's 5 letters (S, P, E, A, R) form one of Scrabble's richest anagram families: 7 valid 5-letter words including PARSE, REAPS, SPARE, PARES, APERS, PEARS, and RAPES. The S tile makes this set especially powerful—S enables hooks onto any existing board word, turning a 5-letter play into a dual-word scoring event.

The SPEAR Anagram Family

SPEAR's five letters form seven 5-letter anagrams—one of the largest anagram families for a 5-tile set. This means that wherever you need to place these tiles on the board, you likely have multiple valid word orientations: SPEAR (7 pts), SPARE (7 pts), PARSE (7 pts), REAPS (7 pts), PARES (7 pts), PEARS (7 pts), and RAPES (7 pts). All score identically—the choice is purely about board position.

The S in this set is crucial. Playing SPARE, PARSE, or REAPS with the S hooking onto an existing board word doubles your scoring: you score SPARE/PARSE/REAPS as a new word PLUS the existing word that gains a new final S. This is why the SPEAR letter set ranks among the top 10 most valuable 5-tile rack combinations in competitive Scrabble.

Shorter Words From SPEAR

Four-letter words: PEAR, RAPE, REAP, PARE, SPAR, APES, EARS, ARES, RAPS, PASE, SEAR, SERA. Three-letter words: APE, ARE, EAR, ERA, PAR, PAS, PEA, PER, RAP, SAP, SEA, SPA, REP. Two-letter: AE, AR, ER, ES, PA, PE, RE.

SPEAR in Wordle

SPEAR is an effective Wordle guess—it tests S, P, E, A, R, covering five common letters including two vowels. Many Wordle players use SPEAR as a second guess after testing other consonant clusters. Variants like SPARE or PARSE are equally effective as Wordle guesses since they test the same five letters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many 5-letter words can you make from SPEAR?
Seven: SPEAR, SPARE, PARSE, REAPS, PARES, PEARS, APERS, and RAPES. All score 7 base points. This is one of the largest 5-letter anagram families in Scrabble, giving you maximum board placement flexibility with these five tiles.
Is SPEAR a good Wordle guess?
Yes. SPEAR tests S, P, E, A, R—five common Wordle letters including two vowels (E and A). It is comparable to SPARE or PARSE as a Wordle guess since all three test identical letters. Use whichever fits your confirmed letter positions from previous guesses.
What is APERS in Scrabble?
APERS is the plural of APER (one who apes or mimics), valid in both TWL and SOWPODS. It scores 7 pts and is one of the seven 5-letter anagrams of SPEAR. Like all SPEAR anagrams, its S can hook onto an existing board word for dual-word scoring.
How do I use the S tile in the SPEAR set?
The S in SPEAR is your hook tile. Place your 5-letter word (SPARE, PARSE, etc.) so the S hooks onto an existing board noun or verb—SPARE hooks FARM to FARMS, scoring both SPARE and FARMS simultaneously. The S hook is the primary reason the SPEAR tile set is considered top-tier.
What 6-letter words can I make by adding a tile to SPEAR?
SPEARS (+S), REPASS (+S), CAPERS (+C), DRAPES (+D), GRAPES (+G), PAPERS (+P second), TAPERS (+T). Add common consonants to the SPEAR rack to identify 6-letter extension opportunities. The word finder tool above lists all valid extensions automatically.
