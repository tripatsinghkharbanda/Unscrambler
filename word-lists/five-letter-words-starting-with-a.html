Wordle Strategy: A-Starting Words

A appears in ~45% of Wordle answers. Openers like CRANE, STARE, and SLATE all contain A. When A is green in position 1, try ABIDE, AFOUL, AGLOW to test common second letters.

Pro Tip

When A is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: AA, AB, AC, AD, AE. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: A-Starting Words

A is worth 1 point and is one of the most rack-flexible tiles. A-starting plays work well on opening squares that reach Double Word Score positions.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring A-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
