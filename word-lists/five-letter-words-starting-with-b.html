Wordle Strategy: B-Starting Words

B starts fewer common words than expected, making a green B in position 1 highly informative. BLAZE, BRAVE, and BROKE are strong B-starting test guesses.

Pro Tip

When B is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: BA, BE, BH, BI, BL. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: B-Starting Words

B scores 3 points. BLAZE (16pts) and BANJO (14pts) are high-value B-words for premium squares.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring B-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
