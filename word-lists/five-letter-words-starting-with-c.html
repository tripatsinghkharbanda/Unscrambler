Wordle Strategy: C-Starting Words

CRANE and CHART are two of the best Wordle openers. A green C with known vowels quickly narrows to CHEST, CHILD, CLAIM, or CLEAN.

Pro Tip

When C is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: CA, CE, CH, CI, CL. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: C-Starting Words

C scores 3 points. CH- and CR- clusters produce high-frequency words. CC pairs (OCCUR) score well on premium squares.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring C-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
