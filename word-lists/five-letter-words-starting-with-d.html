Wordle Strategy: D-Starting Words

DEALT and DREAM are popular D-starters that test common letters. After confirming D, target DA-, DE-, DI-, DR- patterns next.

Pro Tip

When D is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: DA, DE, DH, DI, DJ. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: D-Starting Words

D scores 2 points. The -ED ending converts almost any verb to a D-ending word, but D-starting plays include DIZZY (24pts) on premium squares.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring D-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
