Wordle Strategy: E-Starting Words

E is in 56% of Wordle answers but starts far fewer. A green E in position 1 narrows the field significantly — test vowel clusters EA-, EI-, EU- in your next guess.

Pro Tip

When E is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: EA, EB, EC, ED, EE. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: E-Starting Words

E is worth 1 point. E tiles pair with J, Q, X, Z to form JEE, QUA, EX, ZEE — all useful Scrabble two- or three-letter words.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring E-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
