Scrabble Strategy: H-Starting Words

H scores 4 points. Two-letter H-words (HA, HE, HI, HO) are essential for parallel Scrabble plays in tight board positions.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring H-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
