Wordle Strategy: I-Starting Words

IRATE is a popular Wordle opener testing I + 4 high-frequency letters. Green I in position 1 is rare — leads to IRKED, IVORY, and ISSUE as strong follow-up guesses.

Pro Tip

When I is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: IA, IC, ID, IG, IH. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: I-Starting Words

I scores 1 point. Excess I tiles weaken Scrabble racks — use IMAGE, INEPT, and INFER to clear multiple I tiles at once.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring I-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
