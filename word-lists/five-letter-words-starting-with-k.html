Wordle Strategy: K-Starting Words

K starts very few Wordle words, making a grey K high-information. KNIFE, KNOCK, and KNEEL are the most common K-starting Wordle answers.

Pro Tip

When K is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: KA, KB, KE, KH, KI. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: K-Starting Words

K scores 5 points. KA (SOWPODS) and KI (SOWPODS) are two-letter K-words for parallel plays. KLUTZ (16pts) scores well on premium squares.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring K-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
