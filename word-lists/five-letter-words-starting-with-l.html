Wordle Strategy: L-Starting Words

LANCE, LEAPT, and LEMON are strong L-starting Wordle guesses. L is common in position 3-4 of Wordle answers — a grey L in position 1 doesn't eliminate much.

Pro Tip

When L is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: LA, LE, LI, LL, LO. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: L-Starting Words

L scores 1 point. -LY and -LE endings convert many words into new plays. JAZZY-style words adding L near premium squares score high.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring L-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
