Wordle Strategy: M-Starting Words

METAL, MERIT, and MOURN are common Wordle answers. M is moderately common — appears in ~15% of all answers in any position.

Pro Tip

When M is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: MA, MB, ME, MI, MO. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: M-Starting Words

M scores 3 points. MAGIC on a Double Word Square scores 12 base points. MUZZY (21pts) and JAZZY with M-hooks score extremely high.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring M-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
