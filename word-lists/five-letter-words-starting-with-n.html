Wordle Strategy: N-Starting Words

NERVE, NIGHT, and NOBLE are useful N-starting guesses. N is much more common as a word-ender than a starter in Wordle answers.

Pro Tip

When N is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: NA, NE, NG, NI, NO. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: N-Starting Words

N scores 1 point. NA, NE, and NO are essential two-letter plays. N forms many bingo patterns: adding N to 6-letter racks frequently produces 7-letter bingos.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring N-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
