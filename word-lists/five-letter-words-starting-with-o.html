Wordle Strategy: O-Starting Words

OCEAN, ONSET, and OLIVE test O in position 1 alongside common consonants. O is the second most common vowel in Wordle, so a green O in position 1 is a significant narrowing.

Pro Tip

When O is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: OA, OB, OC, OD, OF. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: O-Starting Words

O scores 1 point. OOH, OOT, OOF (SOWPODS) clear excess O tiles. OUZO (14pts) is one of the highest-scoring 4-letter O-starters.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring O-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
