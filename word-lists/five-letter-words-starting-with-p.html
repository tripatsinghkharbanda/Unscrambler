Wordle Strategy: P-Starting Words

PARSE, PEARL, and PLAIN are strong P-starting Wordle guesses. PL- and PR- patterns appear frequently in common English words.

Pro Tip

When P is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: PA, PE, PH, PI, PL. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: P-Starting Words

P scores 3 points. PA, PE, PI are valid in TWL and SOWPODS. PIZZA (24pts) and PROXY (17pts) are high-value P-words for premium plays.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring P-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
