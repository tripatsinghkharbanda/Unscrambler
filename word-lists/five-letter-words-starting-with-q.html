Wordle Strategy: Q-Starting Words

Q is the rarest Wordle starting letter. QUEEN, QUERY, and QUIET are nearly all the Q-starting answers. A grey Q eliminates almost nothing — but a green Q almost solves the puzzle.

Pro Tip

When Q is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: QA, QO, QU. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: Q-Starting Words

Q scores 10 points (highest, tied with Z). Place Q on a Triple Letter Score for 30 points from Q alone. QUIXOTIC (rare) and QUARTZ (24pts) are spectacular premium-square plays.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring Q-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
