Wordle Strategy: R-Starting Words

R is in 43% of Wordle answers. RAISE and CRANE are among the best openers specifically because of R's frequency. A green R in position 1 leads to REACT, REALM, RISKY, and ROUND.

Pro Tip

When R is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: RA, RE, RH, RI, RO. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: R-Starting Words

R scores 1 point. -ER, -AR, -OR endings convert almost any root to a new word. R is essential in bingo words and hooks onto hundreds of board positions.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring R-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
