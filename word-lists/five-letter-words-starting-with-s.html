Wordle Strategy: S-Starting Words

S starts more 5-letter words than any other letter. SLATE and STARE are among the best Wordle openers. Note: NYT Wordle avoids S-plural answers, so while S-words are valid guesses, plurals rarely appear as answers.

Pro Tip

When S is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: SA, SC, SE, SH, SI. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: S-Starting Words

S scores 1 point but is the most tactically powerful Scrabble tile. An S can pluralise almost any noun on the board. Save S tiles for bingos or hooks on high-value board positions.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring S-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
