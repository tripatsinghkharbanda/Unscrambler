Wordle Strategy: T-Starting Words

TRACE, TEARS, and THORN are common T-starting Wordle words. T is the third most common consonant — testing T in position 1 returns useful information without being overly restrictive.

Pro Tip

When T is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: TA, TE, TH, TI, TO. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: T-Starting Words

T scores 1 point. TA, TE (SOWPODS), TI are two-letter T-words for parallel plays. TOPAZ (16pts) and FRITZ-style T-words score well on premium squares.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring T-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
