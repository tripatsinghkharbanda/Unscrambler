Pro Tip

Scrabble Strategy: W-Starting Words

W scores 4 points. WALTZ (17pts) and WHIZZ (29pts) are among the highest-scoring W-words. Two-letter WO (SOWPODS) allows useful parallel plays.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring W-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
