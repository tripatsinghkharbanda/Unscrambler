About 5-Letter Y-Words

Y starts a small set of 5-letter words: YACHT, YIELD, YOUNG, YOURS. Y scores 4 points in Scrabble and is more useful as a word ending (-LY, -RY) than as a starter, but knowing Y-words helps clear difficult racks.

Wordle Strategy: Y-Starting Words

Y is far more common as a word-ending than a starting letter in Wordle. YACHT, YIELD, YOUNG, and YOURS are the main Y-starting answers.

Pro Tip

When Y is confirmed green in position 1, your next guess should test the most common 2nd-letter patterns: YA, YE, YI, YL, YO. This narrows the answer set fastest.

Scrabble Strategy: Y-Starting Words

Y scores 4 points. Adding Y to a noun or adjective on the board (EARL→EARLY, HORN→HORNY) is a key Scrabble tactic. YEH, YEP, YEW are 3-letter Y-words for tight board positions.

  1. Target premium squares: Play your highest-scoring Y-word on a Double or Triple Word Score square first.
  2. Parallel plays: Place a 5-letter word alongside an existing word to score both simultaneously.
  3. Rack balance: A 5-letter play leaves 2 tiles, so you draw 5 new tiles — choose words that keep a good vowel-consonant mix.
