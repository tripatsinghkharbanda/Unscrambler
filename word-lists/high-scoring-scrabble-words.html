How Scrabble Scoring Works

Each Scrabble tile has a face value from 1 (A, E, I, O, U, L, N, S, T, R) to 10 (Q, Z). The base score of a word is the sum of all tile values. Premium squares multiply individual tiles (Double/Triple Letter Score) or entire words (Double/Triple Word Score).

Words with high base scores contain multiple high-value tiles. Q (10pts), Z (10pts), J (8pts), X (8pts), and K (5pts) are the five highest-value tiles. Words combining two or more of these tiles score dramatically higher than average.

Strategy

The highest base-score word in your rack is rarely the best play. Always consider which premium squares are available and whether a lower-scoring word reaches a Double or Triple Word Score square for a bigger total.

High-Value Tile Combinations

  • Q + Z words: Extremely rare. QUIZ scores 22 base points and is one of the most efficient 4-letter plays in Scrabble.
  • J + X words: JINX scores 18 base points — excellent on any premium square.
  • Z + J or Z + X: No common English words, but Z alone in JAZZ (29pts) or FIZZ (25pts) scores very high.
  • K combinations: KNACK (13pts), KAYAK (16pts), and KHAKI (14pts) are reliable high-value plays with K.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest base score possible in Scrabble?
The theoretical maximum word score (without premium squares) for a word in the ENABLE dictionary is 44 points — achieved by ZYZZYVAS. Real games rarely see scores above 50 base points without premium squares.
What is the highest score ever achieved in a Scrabble game?
The record Scrabble word score is 392 points for CAZIQUES, placed on premium squares in a professional game. The highest single game score ever recorded is 850 points.
Are all words on this list valid in tournament Scrabble?
All words are from the ENABLE dictionary — the basis for TWL (North American Scrabble). Some words may not be in Collins SOWPODS. Always verify with your tournament's official dictionary.
