Scrabble Strategy for Three-Letter Words

3-letter words are the backbone of Scrabble scoring. They allow you to extend existing words at both ends and score parallel plays. Every competitive Scrabble player memorises all valid 3-letter words.

  1. Premium squares: From the opening square, a 3-letter word can reach a Double or Triple Word Score square on the right board. Always aim for bonus squares first.
  2. Rack balance: A 3-letter play draws 3 new tiles. Choose words that leave a balanced vowel-consonant rack for your next turn.
  3. Parallel plays: A 3-letter word placed alongside an existing word scores both the main word and the new letters it creates by touching the board.
  4. Extension potential: Learn which 3-letter words accept prefix or suffix extensions to set up a longer future play.
Strategy Tip

The most efficient 3-letter Scrabble plays score 15+ base points. On a Double Word Score square, that becomes 30+ — enough to build or maintain a meaningful lead.
