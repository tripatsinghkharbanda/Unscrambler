Words That Start With Q

Q is worth 10 points—tied with Z as Scrabble's highest tile value—but unlike Z it almost always demands a U companion. The two critical Q-without-U words every player must know: QI (11 pts, valid in both TWL and SOWPODS) and TRANQ (14 pts, both dictionaries). These two words make the Q tile unblockable.

The Q Problem—and Its Solution

Most players fear the Q tile because it usually requires U. But the U dependency is overstated. In TWL, QI and TRANQ play without U; in SOWPODS, the list expands to include QANAT, QOPH, QADI, QAID, and others. Memorise QI as your emergency play—two letters, 11 points, requires nothing. QI also extends to QIS (12 pts).

QUIZ is the star of the QU- word list: Q+U+I+Z = 10+1+1+10 = 22 base points in four tiles. On a double-word square, QUIZ becomes 44 points. QUAFF (21 pts) and QUIFF (21 pts) are the next-best short QU- plays. For 5-letter QU- words, QUALM (16 pts) and QUAKY (21 pts) stand out.

Q in Wordle and Other Games

Q appears in under 1% of five-letter Wordle answers. If you draw Q as a yellow tile, valid 5-letter Q answers include QUAFF, QUAIL, QUALM, QUART, and QUEEN. In crosswords, Q almost always appears in the QU__ pattern. QUEUE (Q-U-E-U-E, five letters, four vowels) is a famous crossword entry for its unusual vowel density.

Q + Premium Squares

The highest Q scores come from landing Q on a triple-letter square (TLS). Q on TLS = 30 pts just for one tile. A 3-letter word like QAT (Q on TLS + A + T) scores 32 pts; QUIZ with Q on TLS = 52 pts before any word multiplier. Scan premium squares first whenever you draw Q—if one is available in a Q-word lane, that play is almost always correct.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Q words do not need a U in Scrabble?
In TWL: QI and TRANQ. In SOWPODS: also QOPH, QANAT, QADI, QAID, SHEQEL, QINTAR, QAWWALI. QI is the critical one—11 points, two tiles, no U. QIS (plural) is equally valid. Memorise these and you will never be stuck with an unplayable Q.
What is the highest-scoring Q word?
QUIZ scores 22 base points (Q=10, U=1, I=1, Z=10). QUIZZED scores 34 pts using two Z tiles. On a double-word square QUIZ becomes 44 pts. QUAFF and QUIFF both score 21 pts and are more easily achievable when Z tiles are unavailable.
Is QI valid in Scrabble?
Yes. QI (life force in Chinese philosophy) is valid in both TWL and SOWPODS. It scores 11 points—tied with ZA as the highest-scoring 2-letter word. QIS (plural) is also valid (12 pts). QI is your most important Q emergency play.
What Q words work in Wordle?
Five-letter Q words that are valid Wordle answers: QUAFF, QUAIL, QUALM, QUART, QUEEN, QUERY, QUICK, QUIET. Q appears in fewer than 1% of Wordle answers, so most strategies don't test Q early—only do so if you have a confirmed Q from a previous guess.

Q-Starting Words: When to Use Them and When to Wait

Q-starting words in Scrabble almost always require a U — QUEEN, QUIET, QUICK, QUILL, QUEST — which means their playability depends entirely on whether you hold a U alongside the Q. With Q and U in hand, finding a Q-starting word is usually straightforward because common ones are high-frequency English words. The strategic question is whether to play a moderate-scoring Q-word immediately or hold for a better position. Rule of thumb: if a Q-starting word can reach a Double or Triple Word Score, play it immediately; a QUEEN on a DWS scores 28 base points, which is an excellent turn.

For Wordle, Q in the answer almost always appears in position 1 or 2 (QU- at the start of a word). If your guesses confirm Q is in the answer, test QUEEN, QUERY, QUITE, QUICK, QUAIL, or QUALM as your next guess to place both Q and its almost-inevitable U companion. QUERY and QUEEN cover the most common following vowels (E and U) and are strong candidates for the actual answer.
