Words That Start With Z

Z is tied with Q as Scrabble's highest-value tile at 10 points—but Z is far superior in practice because it plays freely without any companion requirement. ZA (slang for pizza, 11 pts) is valid in both TWL and SOWPODS and is the highest-scoring 2-letter word in the game. ZAX (19 pts, 3 tiles) combines Z and X for exceptional early-game plays.

Why Z Is Better Than Q

Q and Z share a 10-point tile value, but Z is dramatically more playable. Q almost always needs U; Z needs nothing. You can play ZA, ZAG, ZAP, ZAX, ZED, ZEK, ZEN, ZIT, ZOO, and dozens more without special companion tiles. The Z tile, unlike Q, is never "stuck." On a crowded mid-game board, Z always has somewhere to go.

ZA (2 letters, 11 pts) is the crown jewel—valid in both TWL and SOWPODS. ZA on a triple-letter square hitting the Z scores 31 pts from a 2-tile play. In SOWPODS, ZO (a Tibetan cattle hybrid, 11 pts) adds a second 2-letter Z option. ZAX (3 letters, 19 pts) combines Z and X—whenever both appear on your rack, play ZAX on the best available premium square immediately.

Long Z Words and Bingo Opportunities

JAZZ (30 pts) uses both Z tiles and is a legitimate bingo-setup word. JAZZY (34 pts) adds Y. SIZZLE, BUZZARD (21 pts), and DAZZLED (26 pts) are achievable high-Z multi-tile plays. For 7-letter bingos: AMAZING (19 pts), BLAZING (19 pts), and GLAZING (18 pts) combine Z with -ING for bingo-length plays at strong base scores.

Z Words for Wordle

Z appears in under 3% of Wordle answers but occurs in all positions. Valid 5-letter Z answers: ZONAL, ZLOTY, ZINCS, ZONES, ZEBRA. Z in non-initial position: OZONE (pos 3–4), FIZZY (pos 4–5), TOPAZ (pos 5). If your opener yields a yellow Z, immediately test Z in positions 3–5 before position 1—mid-word and final Z is more common in the Wordle answer pool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ZA a valid Scrabble word?
Yes. ZA (slang for pizza) is valid in both TWL and SOWPODS. It scores 11 points for 2 tiles—the highest-scoring 2-letter word in Scrabble. Its plural ZAS (12 pts) is equally valid. Learn ZA and you will never need to pass or exchange a Z tile.
What is the highest-scoring Z word?
PIZZAZZ (using both Z tiles and both blank-supplemented Z positions) scores 48 base points. More practically: JAZZY (34 pts), DAZZLED (26 pts), and JAZZ (30 pts) are achievable. ZAX (19 pts, 3 tiles) is the best short Z play when a premium square is available.
How many Z tiles are in a Scrabble set?
There is 1 Z tile in a standard Scrabble set (10 pts). The limited supply makes Z highly sought after. When you draw Z, immediately scan for ZAX (if X is available), ZA on a premium square, or a JAZZ/JAZZ-family play if both Z tiles have been drawn (which means your opponent has the second one).
What 5-letter Z words can appear in Wordle?
Valid 5-letter Z Wordle candidates: ZONAL, ZONES, ZINCS, ZLOTY, ZEBRA, ZOEAE, ZESTY. Z most commonly appears in positions 1, 4, or 5 in Wordle answers. If you confirm Z as yellow, test it at position 4 or 5 (in words like OZONE or TOPAZ patterns).
Can I use ZO in US Scrabble tournaments?
ZO is SOWPODS-only (valid in UK, Australian, and international tournaments) but not in TWL games (US and Canadian tournaments). Always confirm which dictionary is being used before playing ZO. ZA, however, is valid everywhere.
