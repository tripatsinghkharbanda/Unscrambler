Words With Double Letters

Double-letter words require two identical Scrabble tiles. Some letters have many tiles available (A=9, E=12, I=9, O=8) making double-vowel words common. Others have few (Q=1, Z=1) making double-Q or double-Z words impossible without blank tiles. PIZZA uses both Z tiles. JAZZ uses both Z tiles plus both J tiles—wait, there is only 1 J tile, so JAZZ uses Z+Z.

Which Double Letters Are Possible in Scrabble?

The tile distribution determines which doubles are achievable. With 2 Z tiles, PIZZA (25 pts), JAZZ (30 pts), BUZZ (16 pts), and FIZZ (16 pts) are all achievable double-Z plays. With 2 S tiles, SS words like GRASS (6 pts), PRESS (7 pts), and BLISS (7 pts) are standard. Double-L words (LL) are particularly common because L has 4 tiles: SPELL (9 pts), STILL (8 pts), SHALL (11 pts).

The blank tile transforms double-letter strategy. A blank can represent any letter, so playing PIZZA with a blank as the second Z scores 25 pts minus the Z tile's value (you use 1 real Z at 10 pts + 1 blank at 0 pts = reduced total). More strategically, blanks enable double-letter plays with rare tiles—double-Q is impossible with real tiles but achievable with a blank representing the second Q.

Highest-Scoring Double-Letter Words

JAZZ (30 pts, both Z tiles), PIZZAZZ (48 pts, both Z tiles + bonus positions), BUZZ (16 pts), FUZZY (21 pts), and FIZZY (21 pts) top the double-Z list. Double-X is impossible (only 1 X tile in Scrabble). Double-J is impossible (1 J tile). For more common doubles: SKILL (11 pts, LL), SHALL (11 pts, LL), and KNELL (11 pts, LL) are strong double-L plays.

Double-Letter Words in Wordle

Double-letter Wordle answers are common—roughly 20% of answers have a repeated letter. Famous examples: LEVEL, SWILL, ALLAY, OFFER, ABBEY, ALOFT (wait, no double there), BOBBY, COCOA, EERIE, LLANO, FUNNY, HAPPY, IGLOO, JAZZY, KNEEL, LLANO. If your Wordle guesses have eliminated most letters, suspect a double and test LEVEL, FUNNY, or HAPPY to probe common doubles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the highest-scoring double-letter Scrabble words?
JAZZ (30 pts) and PIZZAZZ (48 pts) lead using both Z tiles. FIZZY and FUZZY (21 pts each) follow. For double-L: SHALL and SKILL (11 pts each). Double-letter words with Z are disproportionately valuable because each Z tile scores 10 pts, and using both tiles doubles the Z contribution.
How many of each tile are in a Scrabble set?
Key counts: A×9, B×2, C×2, D×4, E×12, F×2, G×3, H×2, I×9, J×1, K×1, L×4, M×2, N×6, O×8, P×2, Q×1, R×6, S×4, T×6, U×4, V×2, W×2, X×1, Y×2, Z×2, BLANK×2. Letters with only 1 tile (J, K, Q, X) cannot form doubles without a blank.
Can blank tiles create double letters?
Yes. A blank tile can represent any letter, enabling double-letter plays with rare tiles. Playing PIZZA with one real Z (10 pts) and one blank Z (0 pts) still spells PIZZA and scores 15 pts (P=3, I=1, Z=10, Z=0, A=1). The blank's versatility is why the 2 blank tiles are Scrabble's most valuable tiles despite scoring 0 pts.
How common are double-letter words in Wordle?
Roughly 15–20% of Wordle answers contain a repeated letter. Famous examples: LEVEL, SWILL, EERIE, FUZZY, IGLOO. If your guesses have eliminated many letters without finding the answer, suspect a double and test words with repeated common letters (L, S, E, O) first.
What double-letter words should every Scrabble player know?
Essential double-letter plays by category: Double-Z (JAZZ, BUZZ, FIZZ, PIZZA), Double-L (SPELL, SKILL, SHALL), Double-S (GRASS, PRESS, CROSS), Double-N (INNER, FUNNY, PENNY), Double-E (STEEL, WHEEL, KNEEL). Focus on double-Z first—those plays score the highest by a significant margin.
