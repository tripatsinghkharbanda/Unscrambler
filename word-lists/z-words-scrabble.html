Why Z Words Matter in Scrabble

Z is tied with Q as the highest-value Scrabble tile at 10 points. There is only one Z tile in the bag, and knowing every word that uses it is essential for maximising its value.

Common Z-words include ZAP, ZEN, ZIP, ZIT, ZOO (3-letter), ZEAL, ZEST, ZINC, ZONE, ZOOM (4-letter), and JAZZY, FIZZY, FUZZY (5-letter). Short Z-words (ZA, ZO in SOWPODS) are critical for tight board positions.

Top Tip

ZA (SOWPODS) and ZAP are the shortest Z-words. ZAX (19pts), JAZZ (29pts), and FIZZ (25pts) are the most explosive Z plays on premium squares.

How to Play the Z Tile

  1. Triple Letter + Double Word: Z on a Triple Letter Square inside a Double Word Score play scores 30+ from Z alone, then doubles the total.
  2. Two-letter Z words: ZA (SOWPODS) allows Z to be played in almost any tight position for 11 base points.
  3. Hook words: Learn which words accept Z as a hook: ADZE→ADZES, QUIZ→QUIZZ (invalid) — and which don't.
  4. Bingo bonus: If you can form a 7-letter bingo using Z, you earn 50 extra points on top of the word score.
