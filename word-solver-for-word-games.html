Dictionary Coverage

The word solver searches a combined dictionary of over 370,000 English words, drawing from three open-source word lists:

  • ENABLE (~173k words) — the standard open-source Scrabble reference for US/Canada play, covering all common game-valid words.
  • SOWPODS/Collins extended (~280k words) — adds international Scrabble words valid in UK, Australia, and worldwide tournaments.
  • General English (~370k words) — comprehensive coverage for non-game contexts including educational and creative writing use.

After your first visit, the full dictionary is cached locally in your browser. All subsequent searches run instantly, even without an internet connection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this word solver for Scrabble-style games?

Yes. Select "Scrabble-style" from the Game Mode filter and the tool automatically sorts results by Scrabble tile score and highlights 7-letter bingo plays. The dictionary covers both TWL (US/Canada) and SOWPODS (UK/Australia/international) words. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by any game publisher.

Does this word solver work for Wordle?

Yes. Select "Wordle (5-letter)" from the Game Mode filter to show only 5-letter words. Combine with the Starts With, Ends With, and Contains filters to match your confirmed Wordle clues. All matching words appear instantly from a 370,000-word dictionary.

Do I need to download anything?

No download is required. The tool runs entirely in your browser. After your first visit, the full 370,000-word dictionary is cached locally, so subsequent searches work instantly even without an internet connection.

What is Bingo Hunter mode?

Bingo Hunter mode filters results to show only words of 7 letters or more, and labels each 7-letter word with a BINGO badge. In Scrabble-style games, playing all 7 tiles earns a 50-point bonus — Bingo Hunter mode helps you identify these plays instantly from your rack.

Is this word solver free?

Yes, completely free with no account required. The word finder processes all searches locally in your browser — no data is sent to any server.
